Your game saves itself every half minute; Save & exit saves right away. Press on a floating gloop and drag to aim; the arrow shows direction and power. Let go to launch. Matching numbers merge, and a launched gloop that merges with a floating one keeps floating. If the goo pushes both side pads in to the max at the same time, it's game over.
